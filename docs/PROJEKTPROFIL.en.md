# JOLIBEN as a project work sample

[Deutsch](PROJEKTPROFIL.md) · **English**

**Thiemo Gerhardt · Initiator & Product Owner · Dresden area**

JOLIBEN is my independent AI and product development project. Through it, I explore how AI assistance can be translated into traceable workflows: with clear tasks, verifiable results and deliberate human decisions.

## Starting point

A helpful AI-generated text is not yet a dependable workflow. Practical use raises further questions: What context was considered? What exactly needs to be done? What data and costs have been approved? How will the result be assessed? Who decides whether to accept it?

JOLIBEN makes these transitions a focus of product development. The interface should provide support without obscuring responsibility for decisions.

## My contribution

I define the product vision, prioritise requirements and development steps, set boundaries and acceptance criteria, and oversee the review of results. I use AI tools for implementation and technical analysis, and assess the results in the context of the relevant task.

For me, the project brings together product ownership, AI project management, digitalisation and the design of clear workflows. My IT background and entrepreneurial experience help me connect technical possibilities with practical requirements.

## Progress to date

Selected planning, task and UI workflows have been implemented and tried out in the browser. In a separate development workflow, a bounded task progressed through targeted tests, review and human approval to a merge. The fully connected end-to-end workflow and the controlled return of knowledge to the system remain in development.
